## Findings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  1. [P1] Rejecting an old reconcile does not stop its replay.                                                                                                                                                                                            
     /C:/Users/spenc/source/repos/2birds/Assets/Game/Runtime/Player/PlayerMotor.cs:368 returns when the snapshot’s generation is outdated. FishNet still considers the behaviour reconciling and replays movement afterward. If the generation RPC arrives
     before the corresponding respawn snapshot, historical movement runs against the current body without first restoring a baseline. This can cause extra movement, falling, and correction snaps around respawns or ownership changes. Generation       
     rejection needs to account for the entire replay cycle.                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  2. [P2] Shove strength can depend on render frame rate.                                                                                                                                                                                                 
     /C:/Users/spenc/source/repos/2birds/Assets/Game/Runtime/Items/WorldItem.cs:499 divides displacement by the time between rendered frames. Locally predicted rocks have Rigidbody interpolation disabled and move on physics ticks. For a returning    
     rock hitting its remote thrower, a frame can therefore contain a full tick’s displacement over a shorter frame interval—roughly doubling measured speed at 120 FPS with 60 Hz physics. Use motion and timestamps from the same simulation interval,  
     or consistently interpolate the sampled motion.                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  3. [P2] Position smoothing creates a contact-detection blackout.                                                                                                                                                                                        
     /C:/Users/spenc/source/repos/2birds/Assets/Game/Runtime/Items/WorldItem.cs:356 clears contact samples every frame while correction smoothing runs. Consequently, hasContactPose never survives long enough to sweep during that period. A corrected  
     rock can visibly hit the player without a shove; frequent corrections can extend the blackout. Rebase the discontinuity once and distinguish correction displacement from subsequent physical travel.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  4. [P2] Impact behaviour automatically opts in future item types.                                                                                                                                                                                       
     /C:/Users/spenc/source/repos/2birds/Assets/Game/Runtime/Items/ItemDefinition.cs:49 defaults the multiplier to 1, and contact eligibility accepts every awake world item. New items therefore inherit knockback automatically. Worse, host detection  
     accepts their physical colliders while remote detection requires a SphereCollider, producing host/client differences for future box or capsule items. Defaulting the multiplier to zero and explicitly enabling rocks would keep the current scope   
     controlled.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  ## Scope and later limitations                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Drops also shove. There is no thrown-versus-dropped distinction. The working plan explicitly permits this, but your request says “thrown items.” That discrepancy should be resolved before considering the behaviour final.                          
  - Short ricochets can disappear between snapshots. Remote sweeps only know the displayed trajectory. A server-side contact and bounce between 20 Hz snapshots may never reach the victim’s detector. The plan acknowledges this limitation; reliable    
    impact messages cannot recover an impact that was never detected.                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  The shared velocity-change API, source-side multiplier, separate request/confirmation identities, and maximum remaining recovery are sensible foundations for extension.
