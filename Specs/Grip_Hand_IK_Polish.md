I want to dramatically simplify and make more robust the hand IK for items.

Currently, we have 2 different approaches, 1 for first person and 1 for 3rd person.  I think this needs to stay, even though it introduces complexity.  But held items will (and should) look different in first vs 3rd person.  Though a lot of things may have some overlap.

But this authoring is very difficult.  We have a tool that made it a bit easier, but its still a lot of settings, and even with those settings, I don't feel that everything is covered.

Lastly, hand IK doesn't feel stable.  If I author a new hand position (say on a boulder), the hands in 3rd person become very jittery/glitchy, sometimes even extending out the back of the avatar.  I want this to be robust and stable from a presentation perspective.
